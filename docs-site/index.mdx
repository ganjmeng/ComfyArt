Loomi is an **AI design canvas** inside ComfyUI: an infinite canvas for posters, social images, slides, mockups, short videos and audio, plus a design agent that works on that canvas with real tools. Every image, video, audio and 3D model runs on WaveSpeed with your own WaveSpeed Key, and the cost is shown before it runs.
