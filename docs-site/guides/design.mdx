Text and shapes stay editable vectors on the canvas, and PPTX export with editable objects keeps them as text and shapes.

## Rich text

Double-click a text to edit it. With nothing selected inside, the text toolbar changes the whole text; select a few words and the color, font, weight, size, **B**, *I* and <u>U</u> buttons apply only to that selection, so one text can mix styles.

  <img src="/images/ours/en/rich-text.webp" alt="Text toolbar applying bold and color to a selected phrase" />
</Frame>

The toolbar also has alignment, line and letter spacing, stroke, case and decoration. Mixed styles are kept in PPTX export as separate text runs.

## Fonts

The font menu searches every font you can use, grouped as **Brand kits** (fonts uploaded to your brand kits), **Common** and **This computer** (local fonts, after the browser asks for permission). Each name is shown in its own typeface.

## Shapes and fills

**Insert** adds rectangles, ellipses, polygons, stars, lines and arrows. Select a shape and open **Fill** in its toolbar:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ours/en/fill-editor.webp" alt="Fill editor with a radial gradient" />
</Frame>

- **Solid**, **Linear**, **Radial** or **Image** fill.
- Gradients take any number of color stops. Each stop has a position and a color with transparency, so a gradient can fade out (like the soft sun above). Linear gradients have an angle.
- **Blend mode** (multiply, screen, overlay and the other CSS blend modes) mixes the shape with what is under it.

Strokes can sit inside, centered or outside, solid, dashed or dotted. Rectangles have a corner radius.

## Pen and node editing

Press **P** (or pick the pen from **Insert**) to draw a path: click to add corner points, drag to pull out curve handles, click the first point to close the shape, or double-click, press Enter or Esc to finish an open path.

Double-click a path to edit its nodes. Drag a point to move it and drag a handle to bend the curve. Results of boolean operations are paths too.

  <img src="/images/ours/en/pen-nodes.webp" alt="Editing the nodes of a leaf path" />
</Frame>

## Boolean operations

Select two or more shapes and open **Boolean** in the selection toolbar: **Union**, **Subtract**, **Intersect** or **Exclude**. The result is a single new path with the style of the bottom shape; **Subtract** cuts the shapes above out of the bottom one.
