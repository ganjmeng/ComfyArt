## Audio generation

Open the generate menu in the bottom bar (the arrow next to **Image Generation**) and choose **Audio generation**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ours/en/audio-dialog.webp" alt="Audio generation dialog on the Voiceover tab" />
</Frame>

| Tab | What you give it | Model |
|---|---|---|
| **Music** | style tags (genre, mood, instruments, tempo), optional lyrics with `[verse]` / `[chorus]` lines, and a length | MiniMax Music 3 by default, ACE-Step 1.5 optional |
| **Voiceover** | the text to read and a voice: Chinese, American, British or Japanese, female or male. With an audio clip selected on the canvas, the voice list also offers **Clone the selected audio's voice** | Kokoro, IndexTTS-2 for cloning |
| **Sound effect** | a short description, up to 47 seconds | Stable Audio Open |

<Tip>
**WaveSpeed Key and cost**

Music, voiceovers, sound effects and image to 3D: these run on WaveSpeed cloud models, so they need a [WaveSpeed Key](/guides/wavespeed-key). The estimated cost is shown next to **Send** (or on the button) before anything runs, and jobs of $0.50 or more ask you to confirm. See [Models and costs](/guides/models).
</Tip>
Each result is an audio card on the canvas with a play button, a seek bar and its length. Audio files you drop on the canvas become the same cards, and the Agent can make music, voiceovers and sound effects too.

## Image to 3D

Select an image of a single object, ideally on a plain background, and choose **Image to 3D** under **⋯**. The job runs on a WaveSpeed image-to-3D model (see [Models and costs](/guides/models)) and returns a textured GLB file.

For several views of the same object, select two to four images and choose **Multi-view to 3D** on the multi-select toolbar. Left to right, they are used as the front, left, back and right views; it also runs on WaveSpeed.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ours/en/image-to-3d.webp" alt="A mug photo and the 3D model made from it" />
</Frame>

The 3D card turns slowly by itself. Its toolbar can stop the rotation, set a background color, **Export Current View** as an image you can keep designing with, switch to **Interact** to orbit and zoom with the mouse, and download the GLB. 3D files you upload (GLB, GLTF, OBJ, FBX, STL, PLY) get the same card.

A model takes a few minutes to generate. See [Models and costs](/guides/models).
