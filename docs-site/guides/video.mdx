title: "Video"
description: "Animate images, generate clips from frames or references, then edit, extend and upscale them."
---

All video generation uses MiniMax H3 (see [Models](/guides/models#video)). Upscaling runs on core ComfyUI nodes, and the video editor on the bundled ComfyArt Timeline Render node. Every clip comes with sound generated together with the picture.

## Animate an image

Select an image and click **Animate Image**. Describe the motion, or leave the prompt empty for a gentle default motion.

- **Loop** is on by default: the first frame is reused as the last frame, so the clip plays seamlessly.
- The result is an animated image that keeps the original ratio.

## Generate a video

Switch the generator, or the chat mode, to **Video** and pick a mode:

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **First/last frame** | Text only, or start from a first frame and optionally end on a last frame. |
| **References** | Up to 9 images, 3 videos and 3 audio clips: characters, products and scenes, a camera move or action to copy, a voice or sound. In the prompt they are `<Picture 1>`, `<Video 1>`, `<Audio 1>` and so on, numbered per type in the order they were added; say what each one should do. The clip follows those references instead of starting from a fixed frame. |
| **Motion control** | A **Character** image and a **Motion video**. ComfyArt extracts the body pose from every frame of the motion video and the character performs exactly that motion. Picking the motion video sets the ratio and the longest duration that fits it. |
| **Lip sync** | A **Portrait** and a **Speech** clip. The speech becomes the video's soundtrack and the person's lips follow it; the video is as long as the clip. Make the speech with [Voiceover](/guides/audio-3d) or upload a recording. |
| **Storyboard** | Splits one idea into scenes with consistent characters and style, and generates them in order. With the Agent enabled, smart storyboard writes the scenes for you. |

Each clip can be 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4 or 21:9, at 768p or 1080p, 5, 10 or 15 seconds long, with a camera motion preset. See [Generate](/guides/generate#video-generation) for the settings panel.

<Tip>
Reference-to-video follows the prompt closely. Name each reference explicitly ("Use `<Picture 1>` as the product, `<Picture 2>` as the presenter") rather than only mentioning it in passing. A reference video also carries the look of the people in it; to put a different character into someone else's movement, use **Motion control**, which only takes the pose.
</Tip>

## Edit a video

Select a video for its toolbar:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/video-toolbar.webp" alt="Video toolbar above a generated clip" />
</Frame>

- **Crop Video** opens the video editor (see below).
- **Extend Video** continues the clip from its last frame, with an optional prompt for what happens next.
- **Upscale** to 1080p, 2K or 4K. Audio and frame rate are kept.
- **Remove Background** cuts the subject out frame by frame onto black.
- **Remix** runs the same prompt and settings again with a new seed.

Videos can also be dropped into the chat as references for the Agent. The preview lightbox has a mute toggle.

## Video editor

**Crop Video** opens the editor with the selected video as the first clip. It can cut several videos and a music track into one new video.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/video-editor.webp" alt="Video editor with three clips, a music track and beat marks on the timeline" />
</Frame>

- **Clips**: **Add Clip** appends any video on the current page. Drag a clip's edges to trim it, and use **Split** to cut it at the playhead. Select a clip to set its speed (0.5x to 2x) and volume, mute it, **Move Left** or **Move Right**, or delete it.
- **Transitions**: click the point between two clips to pick a hard cut, **Fade**, **Fade to Black** or **Fade to White** and set its length. **Apply to All Transitions** copies it to every cut.
- **Music**: **Add Music** puts an audio element from the page under the whole edit, for example a song made with [audio generation](/guides/audio-3d). Select it to set its volume and **Start at**, the second of the song that plays on the first frame. ComfyArt detects the song's beats and shows the BPM.
- **Snap to Beats**: on by default. Beats show as marks on the timeline, and clicking the timeline moves the playhead to the nearest beat or clip edge, so **Split** cuts land on the beat.
- **Captions**: **Add Caption** places text at the playhead. Set its start, end, font size and color.

Undo, Redo and Reset are in the header. **Export** renders the edit in ComfyUI at the first clip's size and 24 fps, with the clips' audio mixed under the music, then **Export as New Video** places it next to the original or **Replace Original Video** swaps it in. Export uses the **ComfyArt Timeline Render** node that ships with ComfyArt, so it needs no extra model.

Agents can make the same edit with the `edit_video` [MCP tool](/guides/mcp).
