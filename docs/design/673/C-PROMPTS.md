## ppt-visual stitching and seam check
- Stitched example = **4 pages** (#7–#10) from the gold's 10-page strip (p02, p07, p08, p10). The PREMIUM SLIDES card cover (#6) is a separate card image. Pages are cropped from the gold at h=1047 (10470/10) and used as the edit base. Each 1K output is upscaled to width 1920, center-cropped to 1920×1047 and stacked vertically → **1920×4188**.
- If UIUX wants a 5th stitched page (title page p01 "住进山海之间" → new title), that's +$0.04 (→ $0.92). If UIUX decides 4–5 pages don't read as this skill, going back to 10 pages costs about +$0.24 and needs 技术经理's OK.
- **Seam check (free, local):** (1) Render `seams.png`: a 200 px band around each join at 100%, with a red hairline at the join. (2) Per join, record the mean luminance difference between the 8 px rows above and below (target < 18/255, the gold's own joins are the baseline) and confirm there's no stray white or black line (row mean not < 5 or > 250). (3) Confirm the grade stays consistent from page to page (mean L* spread ≤ the gold's). A failing join gets a local 4 px dark divider like the gold's. Nothing is regenerated.
