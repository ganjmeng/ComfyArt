(2) Per join, record the mean luminance difference between the 8 px rows above and below (target < 18/255, the gold's own joins are the baseline) and confirm there's no stray white or black line (row mean not < 5 or > 250). (3) Confirm the grade stays consistent from page to page (mean L* spread ≤ the gold's). A failing join gets a local 4 px dark divider like the gold's. Nothing is regenerated.
